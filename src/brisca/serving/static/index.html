Rules in 30 seconds

Each player holds three cards. The face-up card sets the trump suit. You may play any card: the highest trump wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the suit that was led. Card order is Ace, Three, King, Knight, Jack, then 7 down to 2. Aces are worth 11, threes 10, kings 4, knights 3 and jacks 2. The trick winner draws first and leads next. More than 60 of the 120 points wins.
